# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone. Participants should communicate constructively, assume good faith while evaluating technical claims critically, respect privacy, and make room for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, sexualized conduct, and personal attacks.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, community spaces, and private communication made in the role of a project participant.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through an agreed private channel. Do not include unrelated secrets or personal data. Maintainers may remove content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant based on severity and pattern.

Maintainers will handle reports as privately and consistently as practical. Retaliation against a reporter or witness is prohibited.
